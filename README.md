Food Recognition and Nutritional Estimation

This project uses deep learning to recognize food from images, classify the food into a category, and provide estimated calorie and nutritional information.

Model: MobileNetV2 CNN with Transfer Learning

Food Categories:

Fast Food
Fruit
Vegetable
Nuts
Dessert

Food Classes: 20 specific food classes

Dataset: Internet food images + 30–50 own food images

Output: Food name, food category, confidence score, calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat.
